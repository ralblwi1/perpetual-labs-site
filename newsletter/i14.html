AI's biggest vertical wins share one pattern: proprietary data plus checkable outcomes

Look at where AI has delivered the most value in specific verticals and a single template repeats: a proprietary or hard-to-assemble dataset paired with an objective, verifiable outcome. Protein structure had experimental ground truth; code has executable tests; competition math has checkable answers; finance had a proprietary corpus. The pattern unifies otherwise unrelated breakthroughs and yields a concrete filter for choosing the next vertical to target.

// evidence — extracted from the papers
[01]A proprietary experimental dataset + objective accuracy metric drove the win.
“Trained on experimentally-resolved protein structures, AlphaFold crossed 90% accuracy and outperformed all other approaches at CASP14.”
▸ Highly accurate protein structure prediction with AlphaFold — John Jumper et al., 2021
[02]Scaling on the same structured biological data extended the capability.
“ESM-2 (up to 15B params) saw atomic-level structural information emerge with scale, enabling 600M+ predicted metagenomic structures.”
▸ Evolutionary-scale prediction of atomic-level protein structure with a language model — Zeming Lin et al., 2023
[03]Code's value comes from test-verifiable, real-world tasks.
“SWE-bench uses real GitHub issues whose fixes are verified by tests; the best system resolved only 1.96%.”
▸ SWE-bench: Can Language Models Resolve Real-World GitHub Issues? — Carlos E. Jimenez et al., 2024 (arXiv:2310.06770)
[04]Hard verifiable math remains an open, gradeable frontier.
“Leading models solve less than 2% of FrontierMath despite near-perfect GSM-8k and MATH scores.”
▸ FrontierMath: A Benchmark for Evaluating Advanced Mathematical Reasoning in AI — Elliot Glazer et al., 2024 (arXiv:2411.04872)
[05]A proprietary corpus is the moat in finance.
“BloombergGPT's edge comes from a 363B-token proprietary financial dataset.”
▸ BloombergGPT: A Large Language Model for Finance — Shijie Wu et al., 2023 (arXiv:2303.17564)

why it's non-obvious

These are celebrated results in unrelated fields, never analyzed as one class. Extracting the shared template — proprietary data × verifiable outcome — and turning it into a forward-looking vertical filter is the synthesis.

implication for perpetual labs

This is the operational form of Perpetual Labs' selection lens: score candidate verticals on (1) a proprietary/hard-to-assemble data source and (2) an objective, checkable outcome. Verticals scoring high on both — clinical claims/coding, regulated-industry workflows, lab/test data — are where created data compounds into a defensible asset.

limitations — what this does not show

Scope of the filter. This filter scores only two properties — data-proprietariness and outcome-verifiability. It is explicitly necessary, not sufficient. It does not score AI-automation alpha, vertical monopoly dynamics, AI-integration greenfield, market-entry ease, or competitor data-vendor gaps.
